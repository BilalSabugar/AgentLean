# Code of conduct

AgentLean welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique the work, support
claims with evidence, and help others reproduce problems.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, impersonation,
deliberate disruption and disclosure of another person's private information are
not acceptable. Do not pressure someone to share private logs, credentials or
personal account details to participate.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other spaces used for
AgentLean collaboration. Participants should also avoid using a project role to
harass someone elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the [maintainer](MAINTAINERS.md) privately with the subject
`AgentLean conduct report`. Include relevant links and enough context to explain
the concern. Do not publish private allegations or identifying information in an
issue. Reports involving a maintainer may need the hosting platform's own abuse
reporting process or a separate trusted channel.

Maintainers will consider context, impact and repeated behavior. They may request
a correction, moderate content, restrict participation or remove access. Serious
threats or privacy violations may require immediate restriction. They will share
report details only as needed to investigate or respond; absolute confidentiality
and fixed response times cannot be promised.

Someone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration privately
with new information. Good-faith technical disagreement is not misconduct.
